# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-28)

First release, for Bedrock 1.21.0 and later.

- Breeze UI (resource pack): Breeze colours on the action bar and title
  background, the hotbar and the selected slot; eight subpacks: the game's
  crosshair or a Breeze cross, dot or circle, each with normal or low fire.
- Breeze HUD (behaviour pack): coordinates, facing, time of day and day,
  low durability warnings and effects with time left in the action bar; a
  Breeze menu to switch each part.
